Wake up by moving.

Sunrep is an alarm clock that won't stop until you finish a short set of push-ups, squats or jumping jacks. Your camera counts the reps on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

How it works

  • Set an alarmPick a time and a move. Choose how many reps it takes to wake up.
  • Move to stop itWhen the alarm rings, start the task. The camera counts each rep on your device.
  • Follow-up alarmsIf you dismiss the system alert and go back to sleep, follow-up alarms keep ringing until the task is done.
  • Private by designNo account, no ads, no analytics. Camera frames never leave your phone.
