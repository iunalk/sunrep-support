Frequently asked questions

My alarm didn't ring

Check that notifications and alarms are allowed for Sunrep. On iOS: Settings → Sunrep → Alarms and Notifications. On Android: allow notifications, Alarms & reminders and full-screen notifications, and turn off battery optimisation for Sunrep if your phone has it. Also make sure the device is on and the app hasn't been force-closed.

Does it ring in silent mode or Focus?

Yes. Sunrep uses the system alarm features (AlarmKit on iOS, exact alarms on Android), so alarms ring even when the phone is on silent or in a Focus mode.

I turned off the alarm and fell asleep again

That's what follow-up alarms are for. After the main alarm, Sunrep schedules follow-up alarms every 2 minutes. They stop only when you complete the wake-up task.

The camera isn't counting my reps

Place the phone so your whole body is in view, about 2 metres away, in good light. Avoid strong light behind you. Move at a steady pace and complete each rep fully.

Can I use Sunrep without the camera?

Yes. Tap Continue without camera, or skip camera access. Your phone's motion sensor then counts the reps, so hold the phone while you move.

How do I turn off an alarm in an emergency?

On the task screen, press and hold Hold to stop for 10 seconds. The alarm stops without completing the task; your streak resets.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by your store account. iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sunrep. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the subscription screen in Sunrep and tap Restore purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete my data in the app, or uninstall Sunrep. All data is stored only on your device. See the privacy policy.
