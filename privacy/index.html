Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Short version: Sunrep has no account and does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal data. Camera frames are processed on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.

1. Who we are

Sunrep is developed by İsmet Ünal, an individual developer, who is the data controller for this app. Contact: ismet.unal@icloud.com.

2. Data the app collects

None. Sunrep does not require an account, does not include analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not send your data to us or to any server we operate.

3. Camera

When you start a wake-up task, the camera is used to count your repetitions. Each frame is analysed in real time on your device and released immediately afterwards. Frames, images and video are never stored or transmitted. On iOS, analysis uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. On Android, it uses Google ML Kit pose detection, which also runs on the device. You can use Sunrep without granting camera access; your phone's motion sensor then counts the reps instead. Motion sensor data is also processed only on the device.

4. Google ML Kit on Android

ML Kit processes images on the device. Google states that ML Kit may contact Google servers to send diagnostic and usage information about the SDK itself (for example performance and error metrics). This information does not include camera images. It is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy: ML Kit Terms, ML Kit data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy. This does not apply to the iOS version.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The app only receives confirmation from the store whether a subscription is active.

6. Data stored on your device

Your alarms, settings and task history are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app. Device backups you enable (iCloud or Google) are controlled by Apple or Google under their own terms.

7. Permissions

Sunrep asks for notifications/alarms (to ring on time) and camera (to count reps). You can revoke them at any time in your device settings.

8. Children

Sunrep is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Still, you have rights under applicable law, including the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK, Article 11), the UK GDPR and US state privacy laws (such as the right to know, delete and opt out of sale or sharing). We do not sell or share personal information. To exercise any right or ask a question, email ismet.unal@icloud.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the KVKK Board; in the UK, the ICO).

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

11. Contact
